01

Foundation

Every design decision in this guide has been made according to 4 commitments agreed upon before development was started.

Purpose
Why we exist

To turn financial anxiety into financial confidence for students and young adults without the lecturing or shame of traditional financial apps.

Values
What we believe

Empowerment over judgement: every interaction respects the user. Learning over scolding: mistakes are growth, not failure. Fun over obligation if it isn't rewarding, we haven't designed it right.

Voice
How we sound, always

Your supportive friend who happens to be good with money. Encouraging, never preachy. We talk like a friend, not a banking email.

Tone
How we sound, situationally

Celebration messages are warm and encouraging. Reminders are gentle nudges. Errors are honest and helpful, never blaming.

02

Voice and tone in UI

Voice is the most important tool in the system. A well designed screen can still feel cold if the words are wrong. The examples below demonstrate how we leverage this tool to create a friendly, encouraging learning environment.

+Write it like this

Wins
Nice one! +25 XP for logging.
Specific reward, low-key celebration. The user feels seen, not patronised.
Reminders
Pssst, your phone bill is creeping up.
Playful and low stakes. It makes the user lean in rather than tense up.
Errors
Looks like this amount might be wrong. Try taking another look.
Names the issue, suggests the fix, never blames the user.
Empty states
Nothing here yet. Let's plant something.
Turns a blank screen into an invitation, not a void.

× Never like this

Lecturing
You should reduce your discretionary spending.
Authoritative, scolding, distant. Reads like a financial advisor.
Clinical
Transaction successfully recorded to your account.
Functional but soulless. We are not a bank, so we don't have to sound like one.
Blaming
Invalid input. Please correct and retry.
Cold, vague, and it feels like the user did something wrong.
Over-performing
Slayyy bestie, ur budget is budgeting
Trying too hard. Playful is not the same as performative.
03

Logo and Iconography

The SpendSense mark tells the whole brand story in one motion. A dot launching upwards pushing the two chevrons up to reach new heights, or in our case, new financial goals.

Anatomy of the mark

The chevrons
Two trajectories, one ascent

The upper chevron represents your goals, your future self. The lower chevron represents where you are and the dot is the energy pushing you upwards towards your goals.

The dot
A single deposit, a single decision

The dot represents one action launching you closer to your goals and resetting to do it all over again. Small actions, compounded, lift you up.
